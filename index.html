ABOUT.TXT — Notepad _□×

Hello, visitor.

I'm Drew — a software engineer in Austin, TX. I build AI systems that a whole company uses every day, end to end: architecture, model and data integration, evaluation, deployment, and the operations work that keeps them trustworthy. I've been shipping games in Unity and C# for 13+ years, and I host Nintendrew, a game history channel with 350,000+ subscribers.

These days I'm a senior software engineer on contract with a frontier AI lab. Before that, as Senior Software Engineer II, Applied AI at Pocket Worlds, the studio behind the mobile social MMO Highrise (where I still support the platforms as a contractor), I designed and solely built the two AI platforms the company now runs on: a source-grounded, agentic company assistant with 140+ tools that cites its sources and holds every write to the live game behind human approval, and an in-house generative art suite that trains Highrise's visual style into custom image-edit LoRAs instead of prompting for it. In blind side-by-side votes, the art team picked it over the vendor tool it replaces on 4 of 5 production tools.

My working method is agentic development: I run coding agents in parallel and spend my own time on decomposition, review, and verification. That's how one engineer shipped a production assistant, a model training and evaluation pipeline, an agent that builds internal apps for non-engineers, and autonomous agent experiments in under a year, mostly in stacks I hadn't used before.

On the games side: I co-created and shipped C.A.R.L. to PC and every major console, led development on Draw a Stickman: EPIC 2, and led engineering on Highrise's weekly live-service events. Off the clock, I've spent a decade making Nintendrew the internet's home for retro gaming deep dives, which taught me to explain technical ideas plainly. I also compose, edit, and do voiceover!

Austin, TX. Two cats. One very complete N64 library. Make yourself at home — the guestbook is at the bottom.

RetroSeekerNEW!

2026 · Creator

A retro game hunting app. Point your phone at a cartridge, disc, or box and it identifies the exact release, down to region and edition, then prices it from recent sold listings. Behind it: a catalog of 80,000+ releases across 98 consoles in NA, PAL, and JP, plus a collecting layer with a dex to complete, quests, a map of game stores and swap meets, and trading between collectors.
